## Brand voice

- **Bilingual but Thai-led.** Most prose in Thai. English for technical terms (AI, reconciliation, automation) is fine and expected.
- **Professional but warm.** Not stiff. Imagine explaining to a small business owner, not lecturing.
- **Specific, not vague.** "ลดเวลาทำบัญชี 30–50%" beats "ทำงานได้เร็วขึ้นมาก." Numbers > adjectives.
- **Educational tone.** The reader should leave knowing something new they can apply, not just impressed.

Avoid:
- Hard-sell language ("ดีที่สุด!", "ราคาถูกที่สุด!") — sounds like a banner ad
- Excessive emoji in body prose (one or two per section max; CTA can have a few)
- Vague claims without backing ("AI ช่วยทุกอย่าง" — instead say what specifically)

## Audience defaults

When in doubt, write for: **a Thai accountant in their 30s–50s working at an SME or a small accounting firm.** They know bookkeeping inside-out but are newer to AI and tooling. They want concrete, applicable guidance — not theory.
